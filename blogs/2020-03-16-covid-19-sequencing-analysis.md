SARS-CoV-2, the virus responsible for the disease COVID-19, is now widespread within our communities and has personally affected members and friends of our team. In an effort to contribute however we can, our team has been working to make analysis of genomics data from COVID-19 samples simpler, more accessible, and easier to share with the broader community. Today, we’re announcing a new analysis summarizing the detection of SARS-CoV-2 in samples that contain evidence of the virus.
