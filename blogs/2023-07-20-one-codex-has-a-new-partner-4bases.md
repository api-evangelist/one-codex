We are excited to announce that One Codex is launching a new partnership with 4bases! 4bases is a Swiss based company with an aim to address unmet medical needs in precision medicine, with clinically-validated and cost-effective solutions for healthcare professionals. One Codex’s mission to enable translational applications of metagenomics and microbiome science strongly aligns with this aim.
