One Codex now offers sequencing! Over the years, we’ve heard the same struggles and frustrations from researchers about their microbiome workflow – slow turnaround times, unpredictable costs, high sample minimums, and data that isn’t as reproducible as it should be. We’re excited to share that we now offer shotgun sequencing, including DNA extraction, library prep, sequencing, and bioinformatics on the One Codex platform.
