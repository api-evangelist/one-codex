Sequencing viral genomes from mixed microbial community samples is challenging due to the relatively low quantity of viral reads compared to host and/or background. One Codex and Twist Bioscience have co-developed targeted enrichment-based sequencing and analysis kits for recovery of whole viral genomes from high-background samples 1 . This semi-targeted approach enables enrichment of known viruses while still capturing novel viruses.
