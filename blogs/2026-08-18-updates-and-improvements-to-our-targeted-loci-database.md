We’re proud to announce a major update to our Targeted Loci Database that adds over 19,000 full-length 16S, 18S, and ITS sequences spanning more than 15,000 bacteria, archaea, and fungal species. Additionally, we’ve enriched sequence diversity for over 2,000 exsting organisms, improved accuracy, and made optimizations to maximize species-level detection. With these updates, our Targeted Loci analysis delivers more comprehensive, accurate results, and it’s available now for all One Codex users.
